SmartBudget Manager

SmartBudget Manager is a small Excel file that helps you write down your income and expenses, and tells you if you are still inside your monthly budget.
What it does
    • Records your monthly income and expenses
    • Sorts your expenses into categories
    • Calculates your monthly balance automatically
    • Shows a chart of your expenses by category
    • Warns you when you go over your budget limit
What you need
    • The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx (about 18 KB)
    • Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, Microsoft 365, or a compatible program
    • No internet, no installation and no macros
Get started in 5 steps
    1. Double-click the file to open it. If Excel shows a yellow bar, click Enable Editing.
    2. Go to the Transactions tab (bottom of the window).
    3. In the first empty row, fill in Date, Description, Type and Category. Choose Type and Category from the drop-down lists.
    4. Type the amount in the Income column or in the Expense column, then press Enter.
    5. Open the Dashboard tab to see your totals, balance and chart. Press Ctrl + S to save.
What is inside
Worksheet
What it is for
Dashboard
Total income, total expenses, monthly balance, budget status and the chart
Transactions
Where you enter your income and expenses
Categories
The expense categories and a monthly budget for each one
Good to know
    • Use rows 4 to 50 of Transactions (47 transactions). Rows after that are not counted.
    • Do not type in the Month column or on the Dashboard. They calculate by themselves.
    • Keep one month per file. The totals add every row, so start a new copy each month.
    • The Dashboard shows the first five categories only.
